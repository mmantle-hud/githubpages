# Dr Matthew Mantle

Image from University Website

I am a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and the leader for the BSc(Hons) Information Technology degree. I earned my PhD in 2022, focusing on symbolic AI with a thesis titled "Large Scale Spatial Temporal Reasoning." My ongoing research builds on my AI interests, shifting focus toward the practical applications of Large Language Models. Before joining the university, I worked in industry as a web developer. My teaching is based largely on this previous experience. I teach a number of modules all related in some way databases and web development.
