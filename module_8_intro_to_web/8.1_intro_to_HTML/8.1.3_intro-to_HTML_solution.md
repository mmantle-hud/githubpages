```html
<!doctype html>
<html lang="en">

<head>
    <meta charset="utf-8">
    <title>Tim Berners-Lee</title>
</head>

<body>
    <h1>Tim Berners-Lee</h1>

    <h2>Early Life and Education</h2>
    <p>Tim Berners-Lee is a British computer scientist best known for
    creating the <strong>World Wide Web</strong> in 1989. He studied
    physics at the University of Oxford before beginning a career
    in the technology industry.</p>

    <h2>Work at CERN</h2>
    <p>After graduating, Berners-Lee worked at several technology companies
    before joining CERN, the European particle physics laboratory. It was
    at CERN that he proposed a simple way for researchers to link documents
    together using hypertext.</p>

    <p>His early design included three important technologies:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>HTML for marking up documents.</li>
        <li>URLs for identifying resources.</li>
        <li>HTTP for transferring information between machines.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>The First Website</h2>
    <p>In 1991, the first website went online. It explained how the Web
worked and how others could create their own pages. Berners-Lee later
founded the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), an organisation that
develops open standards to keep the Web free and accessible.</p>
 

    <h2>Awards and Contributions</h2>
    <p>Berners-Lee has received many awards for his contributions to
    computing. He was knighted by Queen Elizabeth II in 2004 and continues
    to work on projects that promote privacy, open data, and a more
    decentralised Web.</p>

    <h2>Key Facts</h2>
    <ul>
    <li>Invented the World Wide Web while working at CERN.</li>
    <li>Created the first web browser and the first web server.</li>
    <li>Founded the W3C to guide the development of web standards.</li>
    <li>Advocates for digital rights and open access to information.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Berners-Lee often emphasises that the Web was designed to be a place
    where <em>anyone can share information</em>. He believes that collaboration and
    openness are essential for the Web to continue benefiting society.</p>
</body>

</html>

```
