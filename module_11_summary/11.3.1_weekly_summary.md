# 11.3.1 Weekly Summary

This week we considered:
- Some relational database and PostgreSQL topics that we haven't had time to consider in detail such as database security, and full text searching. 
- Possible limitations of relational databases such as scalability and schema flexibility. 
- Alternative types of database such as key-value stores, document databases and graph databases. We looked at how they differ from relational databases and why they can offer advantages in some circumstances.
