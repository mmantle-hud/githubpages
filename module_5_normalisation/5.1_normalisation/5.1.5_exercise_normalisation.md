# Exercise: Normalising the Community College Enrolments Database
A small college offer courses to people living in the local community. 
- If local residents sign-up and pay a small annual fee they can become members, this allows them to book onto two courses a year. Optionally, residents can take out 'gold' membership which allows them to book onto an unlimited number of courses. 
- Many courses require members to bring their own equipment e.g. a table tennis bat is needed for table tennis, a laptop for the creative writing course.   

They have a simple database that keeps track of the different courses they offer, the equipment needed and who has enrolled on which course.
